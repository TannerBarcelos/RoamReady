# RoamReady privacy policy

Effective October 27, 2026.

RoamReady helps you plan camping trips and keep your rig details, checklists, documents and memories together. It requires no RoamReady account. It has no advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, or app-operated backend. We do not sell your data.

## Your library

The app stores the information you choose to enter on your device: profile and rig details (including optional VIN and registration), bookings, campground locations, checklists, notes, photos, documents and saved campground lists. Core library features work offline.

In a build configured for iCloud, optional private iCloud synchronization uses your Apple account and Apple's CloudKit service. Apple manages the service, transfers and encryption. The preference can be changed in Profile and takes effect when you next launch the app. Turning sync off does not delete either copy of your library. Apple's privacy terms apply to its services.

## Location and online services

Location access is optional and requested when you choose Home weather or Camp Finder's Near me. The app requests approximate location by default. For local weather, coordinates are rounded to about one kilometer before they are sent to Apple Weather. Camp Finder sends the search area and search terms to Apple Maps. RoamReady does not save your current location in your library. A campground's location is saved when you choose it for a trip; cached weather is kept for offline use.

Apple Maps discovery and fresh weather need a connection. Opening a campground's website, booking link, directions, or a document link uses the corresponding external service, whose privacy practices apply. Saving a linked document offline downloads it from its host.

## Photos, camera and files

Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a photo. The system Photos picker grants access to the items you select, without unrestricted access to your photo library. File imports use the files you choose in the system picker. Cloud-only photos or files may first need downloading through their provider.

Trip and rig photos are resized for storage. Imported documents retain their original bytes. Exporting or sharing creates readable copies outside the app; their storage and sharing destinations are your choice.

## On-device Assistant

Assistant uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models on eligible devices. RoamReady does not send your questions or checklist context to a remote AI provider. The conversation stays in memory across closing the Assistant and clears when you start a new chat or quit the app. Conversations are not saved in the library or synchronized through iCloud. Apple's initial model download may require a connection.

## Control and deletion

You can edit or delete library content in the app. Deleting a trip removes its photos, checklists, notes and rating; its documents become unattached so you can keep or delete them separately. Original items in your system photo library are not deleted.

Profile offers an export and **Erase all data**. In a configured iCloud build, erasing deletes the app's private cloud zone before removing the device library, caches and settings; it needs a connection and an available iCloud account. Other devices can retain local copies and should be erased separately if desired. Copies you exported or shared must be removed at their destinations.

## Support and updates

For privacy questions or support, use [RoamReady's support tracker](https://github.com/TannerBarcelos/RoamReady/issues). Include the device/OS and a description of the problem; do not attach private documents or registration details to a public issue.

Material changes to the app's data practices will be reflected in this policy. The effective date above identifies this version.
