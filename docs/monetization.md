# Pricing

[Documentation index](README.md) · [Release plan](integration-plan.md)

RoamReady is a **$4.99 USD one-time purchase**, paid upfront on the App Store. This is the permanent pricing model, not a launch offer.

- **Everything is included.** Trips and bookings, checklists, documents, memories, iCloud sync, weather, Camp Finder, and the on-device Assistant all work after installation, with no paywall.
- **No subscription, free trial, in-app purchase, or unlock**, now or in later versions. Future features, including any remote AI (#34), must fit this model.
- **No StoreKit code.** The price is set in App Store Connect; the app has no purchase flow.
- **Reinstalling** through the purchasing Apple Account uses Apple's normal redownload; there is no custom Restore Purchases.
- **Listing copy:** "$4.99 one-time purchase. No subscription." Review local prices for each storefront.

App Store Connect setup (Paid Apps Agreement, banking and tax, the price schedule) is tracked in #6.
